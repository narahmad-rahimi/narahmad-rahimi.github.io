About Me

From Balkh to Bern — a journey in technology

I’m an IT apprentice at CsBe Bern in Switzerland, training to become a certified Informatiker EFZ specialized in Platform Development. My path here wasn’t a straight line — I hold a Bachelor’s in Economics from Balkh University, worked in operations and customer service in Afghanistan, and migrated to Switzerland in 2023.

I’m fascinated by how IT infrastructure makes everything work behind the scenes — servers, networks, the systems that keep organizations running. In my free time I build and tinker with my own homelab, programming microcontrollers and sensors. I believe in learning by doing.
